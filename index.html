Controls
  • Run: jump across each sector's star map to the gate. Fights, wrecks and signals give credits and blocks; shops buy, sell, upgrade and repair. Raiders and signals can also turn up mid-jump. Every third sector ends in a flagship or starbase. Lose the core and the run is over.
  • Build: the drydock is 3D. Click a face of your ship to put a block on it, or drag to paint several. Right-click removes a block back into cargo. Right-drag (or two fingers) turns the view, the wheel zooms. Hull and armor plating are bought with credits. Blocks must touch your ship.
  • Damage: blocks destroyed in a fight stay damaged (dark, cracked) and don't fly until repaired. Removing a damaged block scraps it.
  • Facing: R or the Rotate button sets the next block's facing: fore, starboard, aft, port, up or down. Click a placed block of the selected type to turn it. Turrets cover a cone around their facing. Lasers and railguns fire along it and can swing 5° to aim.
  • Engines push toward their facing; the cell behind must be open for the exhaust. Your speed in each direction comes from the engines pointing that way, and how fast you get there from thrust against mass: a small hull with big engines is a fighter, a huge armored one a slow, imposing flagship. Gyros make any hull turn faster.
  • Line of fire: guns can't shoot through your own hull, so put them where they can see out. A red ✕ marks a block that can't fire or thrust; a gold mark means a turret is mostly boxed in.
  • Fly: the ship steers toward the mouse cursor: the further from the centre, the harder it turns. W/S thrust, A/D strafe, R/F rise and sink, Q/E roll. On touch, the left thumb moves and the ship turns to its target by itself.
  • Aim: your ship turns its nose toward the cursor. Big ships turn slowly; gyros help. Hold the mouse button or Space to fire. T or the Auto button switches to auto-aim: the ship targets the nearest enemy and fires on its own. Point defense always fires on its own.
  • Energy: weapons use energy to fire and shields use it to recharge. The core makes a little; Generators add more and Batteries store it. Both are safe deep inside the hull. When energy runs out, weapons wait. The gold Energy stat means full fire uses more than you make.
  • Shields each project a bubble that stops enemy shots from every side, protecting blocks up to 2 cells away (3 at Lv2, 4 at Lv3). Overlap them to cover a big hull. If it pops it is down for 5 s, and it recharges after 2.5 s without hits. Enemies carry the same weapons you unlock. A red dashed line means an enemy railgun is about to fire.
  • Touch: left half of the screen is a move stick, right half aims. Without the aim stick the ship aims at the nearest enemy.
  • Drydock view: mouse wheel, pinch, or the − + buttons zoom; middle-drag or two fingers pan; Fit resets. Ctrl+Z or Undo takes back a change. Blueprints keep up to three designs.
  • Hangar bays launch a fighter (1×4 bay) or bomber (2×6 bay) out of their front end. The craft fights on its own, flies home every 30 s to rearm and repair, then launches again. If it is shot down the bay builds a new one, which takes longer.
  • Missiles blow up in a wide blast. MIRVs (2×2 launchers) split into three homing warheads near their target.
  • Flak shells burst near enemy ships: area damage, and the blast clears enemy bullets.
  • Enemies grow point defense that shoots down missiles, shields (orange bubbles; railguns and lasers go straight through), generators that explode when hit and make them fire faster, and carrier bays that launch fighters and bombers. Some sectors end in a starbase instead of a flagship.
  • Damage knocks blocks off. Anything cut off from the core drifts away for the rest of the fight but comes back afterwards.
  • Weapon groups: every weapon sits in one of five groups: under Weapon groups, pick a number and click weapons on the ship. In a fight, 1-5 or the buttons bottom left put a group on auto: it fires on its own at the nearest hostile while the rest fire with you.
  • Camera: mouse wheel, +/− or the HUD buttons zoom the fight view in and out.
  • P pauses.
