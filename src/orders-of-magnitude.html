Controls
  • Build: click or drag to place. Right-click or the Sell tool removes a block for a full refund. Blocks must touch your ship. New blocks unlock as you clear stages.
  • Facing: R turns the next turret, engine, shield, laser or railgun. Turrets cover an arc around their facing. Lasers and railguns fire straight along it.
  • Engines push away from their exhaust, which must be open. Most of your speed comes from the direction your engines face. Off-centre engines also help the ship turn.
  • Line of fire: guns can't shoot through your own hull, so put them where they can see out.
  • Fly: WASD or arrows. Hold Shift to slow down.
  • Aim: your ship turns its nose toward the cursor. Big ships turn slowly; gyros help. Guns fire on their own. T switches to auto-aim at the nearest ship.
  • Shields stop bullets arriving from the side they face, until they break. A red dashed line means a sniper is about to fire.
  • Touch: drag anywhere to fly; aim is automatic.
  • Damage knocks blocks off. Anything cut off from the core drifts away. Lose the core and the stage is lost. Drydock repairs everything for free.
  • P pauses.
